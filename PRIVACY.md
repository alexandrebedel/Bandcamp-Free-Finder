# Privacy policy

Free Download Finder for Bandcamp, a browser extension.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

## In short

The extension collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing to its developer or to anyone else.

## What it reads

- **The Bandcamp page you have open.** On label and artist pages, the extension reads the release grid (each release's address, title, artist and cover) to show the free list and the bands on the covers. This stays in the page.
- **Bandcamp release pages.** For each release in the grid, it downloads the release's page from Bandcamp and reads its public sale data (the price, whether an email address is required, which tracks are free). These requests go only to `*.bandcamp.com`, and are sent without your Bandcamp cookies, so Bandcamp doesn't see them as coming from your account. Like any request from your browser, they show Bandcamp your IP address and browser.

## What it stores

For each release it has checked, the extension keeps the answer (free, name your price, with email, some tracks free, or paid) and the time it was checked, under the release's address, in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). Answers expire after 24 hours, and expired ones are deleted when the browser starts or the extension is updated.

This data never leaves your computer. Removing the extension deletes it.

## What it shares

Nothing. The extension doesn't sell, transfer or disclose any data, and doesn't use it for anything other than showing which releases are free.

## Good to know

Bandcamp pages can tell that the extension is installed, because the code that runs in the page is loaded from the extension.
